BEAT THIS LANDING

Mock-up for docs/BREAKOUT.md: a shared result becomes a challenge. The link carries the day, the score and the two headline numbers, so a friend flies the same conditions and the card compares the two. No server. Shown on the sky crane mission that exists today; parts marked blue are new, everything else is the current interface. The 3D scene is a flat stand-in.

WHAT IS NEW, AND WHAT IS NOT

  • Reused as they are: the cover, the mission runner and its seeded start (misStart(seed)), the result card, the trace, the share line, the clip.
  • New: reading vs from the link; the cover's challenge state; one inset line; the comparison on the card and the clip; the link written with the player's own numbers.
  • A link from an earlier day still works: it carries its day number, so the same conditions are flown as a practice try that does not count towards today.
  • A score in a link can be typed by hand. That is acceptable between friends and is the reason this needs no server; a public ranking would need one (see BREAKOUT.md).
