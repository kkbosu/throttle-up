# Launch kit

What is ready for the launch `docs/BREAKOUT.md` describes, led by the Apollo lunar landing. Nothing here has been
posted anywhere.

## 1. The lead clip

`node tools/lead-clip.mjs` flies today's lunar landing on this PC's GPU and saves the game's own 9:16 mission clip
(720×1280 WebM, about 25 s, 12 MB) to `snapshots/lead-clip.webm`. `PAGE_URL=https://kkbosu.github.io/throttle-up/`
records from the live site, so the footer carries the real address.

What it is and is not:

- **An autopilot flies it** (the one the checks use), not a person. It lands at 2.0 ft/s with 35 s in hand and scores
  769. Say so if anyone asks, or better, replace it: fly the daily yourself and press ▶ CLIP on the result card. A
  human try with a wobble in it is a better lead than a clean machine one.
- **It is 25 s, not 10.** The last 10 s (dust, touchdown, the score) are the part to post; trim it in the app you post
  from. The game cannot yet start a clip late.
- **It is WebM.** TikTok, Reels and Shorts want MP4. A clip made on a phone is MP4 already; this one needs converting,
  and no converter is installed on this PC.

## 2. Short video and X: captions

One line each, to go with the clip. Pick one; they are drafts.

- Apollo 11 touched down at about 1.7 ft/s, 20 to 30 seconds before the land-or-abort call. Same engine, same site. Your turn.
- The lunar module's engine could only run between 10 and 60% for the landing. You get the same lever the crew had.
- I landed on the Moon at 2.0 ft/s. Beat it: kkbosu.github.io/throttle-up/lunar-module/daily/
- Every number in this lunar landing comes from a NASA report. The flying is up to you.

For X, the challenge link from your own try (SHARE on the result card) is better than the plain one: it opens on
"769 POINTS TO BEAT".

## 3. Hacker News and Reddit: write these yourself

`docs/BREAKOUT.md` (section 2) records that Show HN asks for no generated text in posts and that r/space is reported to
ban AI-generated content. So there is no draft post here on purpose. These are the facts and the order to put them in;
the words should be yours.

**Show HN**

- Title shape: `Show HN: Throttle Up – run real engines in the browser, every fact sourced`. Link the home page, not the
  daily.
- First comment, in your own voice: what it is (ten machines, each one's INFO lists what is sourced and what is
  approximated), why you made it, what is simplified, and what you would like checked. Readers there will open a
  source; say which machine you are least sure of.
- Say up front: 3D needs WebGL2; there is a 2D fallback for three machines; phones are less tested than desktop;
  the sound is synthesized.
- Be there for the first two hours to answer.

**r/space** (ask the moderators before posting): the lunar module or the sky crane, with a descriptive title, no score.

**r/aviation** (rules not checked): the SR-71's inlet spike or the NK-12, not the Harrier, which is still drawn by eye.

**r/InternetIsBeautiful**: reported to bar web games. Post it as the explainer it is; do not mention the daily.

**Facts you can state, with where they come from** (all in `docs/SOURCES.md`):

- The lunar module's descent engine: 10,500 lb rated thrust, throttled between 10 and 60% for the landing (NASA TM
  X-58040; NASA TN D-7143).
- The site is a model of Tranquility Base: West, Little West and Double craters at their sourced sizes and distances,
  the Sun 11.4° up in the east as it was.
- The lever is the rate-of-descent switch the crews flew with: one click is 1 ft/s.
- Stated in INFO as approximations, so do not claim them as fact: crater bearings and depths, the 10° lean, the 12°
  slope limit, the craft's scale.

## 4. Before posting

1. Analytics is not in yet: without it the launch cannot be read.
2. Nobody has flown the lunar landing on a real phone, and SHARE with a clip has never been tried on one.
3. A real flight to time it to, if one is near.
